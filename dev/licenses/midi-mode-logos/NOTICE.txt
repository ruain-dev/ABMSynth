ABMSynth transformation:
Only the four logo outlines are included in assets/midi-mode-logos.json.
Source drawing indices and original origins/bounds are retained in the data.
Original lines and cubic Beziers are preserved;this is not pixel tracing.
Paths are translated to local coordinates. Transparent counters preserve
original white cutouts through winding direction. Theme colours replace ink
colour. One uniform scale fits each original aspect ratio without stretching.
The manuals and the extraction tool are not runtime product resources.

These outlines replace the retired16x16 midi-db LCD approximations.
